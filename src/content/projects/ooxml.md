[ooxml](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/ooxml) is an Apache-2.0 TypeScript library that reads, renders, edits and writes PowerPoint and Word files in the browser, with viewers for React, Vue, Svelte, Angular and plain JavaScript. I have contributed to it since September 2026, first in [pptx-viewer](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/pptx-viewer), the PowerPoint viewer that later moved into ooxml.

## Focus areas

- **Editing fidelity:** keeping text, lists, tables, charts and SmartArt intact through edits and saves.
- **Real-time collaboration:** Yjs-based co-editing that stays in sync across clients.
- **Cross-framework parity:** the same options and behaviour in every viewer binding.
- **Interaction and rendering:** zoom, pan, selection handles, fonts and slide rendering.
- **Performance:** smaller bundles and lazy loading for faster deck loads.
- **Embedding controls:** options that let host apps limit external content, fonts and load size.
